Custom bottle mould development

A customer-specific packaging project for a contract manufacturing client: designing and producing custom bottle moulds. I was the link between the client, the external mould manufacturer and our production team.

What I did

  • Gathered the bottle design requirements from the client.
  • Worked with the mould manufacturer to turn the concept into technical drawings.
  • Ran the drawing review and approval between client and manufacturer.
  • Followed up on pilot mould development.
  • Coordinated production trials on blow moulding and filling machines.
  • Managed the move to full mould production after client approval.

How it progressed

  1. Requirements from the client
  2. Technical drawings with the mould maker
  3. Review and approval of drawings
  4. Pilot mould development
  5. Production trials on blow moulding and filling machines
  6. Full mould production after client approval
